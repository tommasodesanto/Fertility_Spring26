Suggested opening: “Consider a young owner at a binding housing limit, with positive saving and interior old choices.” This is a clarification, not an equation change. The Pareto result is a particular stationary construction, requiring \(MV^Y>u\), \(\lambda<1\), complete initial-title accounting, and outside finance; it does not establish one-time-gift efficiency. Source: constrained-efficiency note 20–33, 44–105, 231–249.
