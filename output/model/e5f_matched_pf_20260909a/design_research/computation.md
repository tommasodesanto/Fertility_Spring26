## What must be recomputed

Write the ten structural coordinates as \(\theta\), the initial preference intercept as \(\psi_0(\theta)\), and the announced change as \(\Delta\psi\). The maintained path is \(\psi_t=\psi_0+\Delta\psi\min\{1,(t-2007)/16\}\) from 2007, held flat after 2023.

| Object | Structural coordinate changes | Only shock amplitude changes |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-announcement stationary household policy, price and distribution | Re-solve, including the initial fertility-intercept normalization | Reuse only if structural parameters, initial anchor and all other primitives remain identical |
| 2007 age reweight, entry normalization and dated supply anchor | Reconstruct under the same approved recipe from the new old state | Reuse the same initial state and supply anchor |
| Terminal household values, stationary population and terminal price | Re-solve at the resulting terminal preference | Re-solve because \(\psi_0+\Delta\psi\) changes |
| Dated household choices and distributions | Recompute full backward/forward path | Recompute full backward/forward path, including choices in 2007 under immediate announcement |
| All dated market-clearing prices | Re-root | Re-root |
| Source data, external schedules, target definitions and weights | Hold fixed within an approved contract | Hold fixed within that same contract |

The initial stationary *distribution* predates the announcement. The 2007 *choice rule and price* generally do not equal their pre-announcement stationary counterparts: households already anticipate the new path. An initial 2003–2006 stationary measurement and a measurement of 2007 choices therefore differ conceptually.

Planning implications, explicitly conditional on similar runtime and successful roots:

- An initial-SS ten-coordinate central probe panel is 21 normalized stationary candidates. At the observed four old-GE solves/candidate, that is about 84 GE solves and **1.7 core-hours**, potentially one roughly five-minute numerical wave on 21 available workers after startup. This is a local sensitivity panel, not a finished calibration; the proposed female normalization may need different iterations or fail to bracket.
- One fixed-price 100-date parameter probe costs about **42–56 minutes**, plus roughly six minutes for a new terminal and, for structural changes, about five minutes for old normalization. It is a conditional derivative, not an equilibrium target evaluation.
- A successful three-path warm-started PF candidate would cost roughly **2.2–2.9 hours including terminal rebuilding**, or slightly more when old normalization also changes. Three calls allow only one new Newton/Broyden trial and cannot guarantee convergence. The initial 100-date root required nine total evaluations across two rounds; the latest continuation speed must not be assumed for every node.
- Five independently rooted scalar-shock trials would therefore be roughly **11–15 core-hours** under that optimistic three-path assumption, plus selection/replay and any failed roots. Parallel trial amplitudes can reduce wall time. A sequential bracket cannot promise that reduction, and monotonicity of the chosen equilibrium fertility statistic has not been verified.
- A central 11-coordinate joint PF panel has 23 candidates. At three paths each it is about **51–68 core-hours**, plus changed-state construction and any extra iterations. With 23 workers that can be several hours rather than days, but it still presupposes a tested candidate loop and effective warm starts. This is one derivative round, not full estimation.

Do not build a fresh 100-column price Jacobian for every candidate by default: each such panel is 20,000 Bellman calls, about **70–93 core-hours** at observed path speeds. The existing price Jacobian is useful numerical information, but the current root contract requires the same selected/normalized/terminal hashes. Reuse across changed parameters needs an explicit, tested *approximate preconditioner* contract, a fresh mapping and final replay; it cannot be passed off as the measured Jacobian of the changed economy. The generic safeguarded Broyden operator already accepts an initial matrix, while the production-facing receipt guards intentionally restrict what may be supplied.

## What can realistically be ready today

**Two-stage design:** a concrete initial objective and tested observer receipts can be made reviewable today, followed by a small stationary sensitivity or fit exercise if the author approves its target assignments. The cheap first stage is attractive. But the seven currently proposed early/structural nonhousing restrictions—early childlessness, one-child share, two timing moments, early wealth ratio, bequest flow and old wealth dispersion—do not identify ten free structural coordinates by count alone. Even adding the reviewed first-birth housing response leaves fewer rows than coordinates. Initial housing/tenure restrictions, explicit external restrictions, or an approved stationary interpretation of modern pooled housing rows are required. Fitting modern transition observations in that first stage instead requires the transition and removes its main speed advantage. Later observations cannot silently become validation-only to obtain a cheap estimator.

**Joint PF design:** the economic solver has now reported a converged inherited-parameter 100-date path. Today can deliver verified candidate plumbing, the new observation contract and a bounded first local panel if those prerequisites close; a fully certified new calibration today is not supported by the evidence. The joint design correctly lets later and pooled observations discipline structural parameters as well as the shock. It remains the coherent implementation of the present empirical proposal.

For either design, first verify the new continuation and resolve horizon assessment without relaxing the rent gate. A controlled extension must preserve every common-date solved price and preference when forming its initial comparison, append only the tail, and then re-root if extended markets fail. Reinterpolating the entire price guess would confound a boundary test. Compare all historical moments and prices and retain every terminal gate. The exact required horizon and runtime of that extension are not yet measured. No parameter search should be described as final while this numerical and empirical work remains open.

## Addendum: the fertility anchor also closes demographic accounting

Changing the initial fertility anchor is **not just changing the scalar normalizer**. In the current [initializer, lines 183–209](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/e5f_matched_pf_initial_state.py:183), 2.1 also sets the effective conversion from births to future household entrants, initializes four waiting birth vintages, and supports an additional stationary-flow gate. Let \(C\) be top-code-adjusted births per model period, \(E\) old household entry, \(s\) the inherited outside-origin entry share, and \(\nu\) the conversion. The exact [renewal helper](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/run_e5f_open_population_transition.py:302) defines

\[
\nu=1/2.1,\qquad B=\nu C,\qquad M=sE,\qquad
\rho=(E-M)/B,\qquad E=M+\rho B.
\]

The helper requires \(0\leq\rho\leq1\). Separately, the initializer requires \(|B/E-1|\leq5\times10^{-4}\), as well as the accounting identity. Consequently merely recomputing \(\rho\) can satisfy the identity and still fail the independent \(B/E\) gate. Substituting an empirical female **period** TFR into \(\nu=1/\mathrm{TFR}\) has no automatic justification: \(C/E\) is births per entrant household under the model's stationary accounting, not that empirical rate. Even stationary terminology does not resolve the exposure and survival differences. The initializer's adjusted birth units are constructed by the [top-code renewal helper](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/audit_closed_reproductive_closure.py:272), which distinguishes explicit children, the 3+ parity weight and a maintained maturation yield.

A new initial-rate contract therefore must explicitly settle: (i) the female-rate observer and initial intercept restriction; (ii) the status/source of \(\nu\), separately from the core child-maturation/entrant-conversion parameters; (iii) the old entry scale \(E\), outside share \(s\), derived outside flow \(M\), and admissible retention \(\rho\); (iv) whether the additional \(B/E=1\) normalization remains substantively intended and how it can coexist with the new fertility anchor; (v) adjusted versus raw birth units, queue length and all initial queue vintages; and (vi) the resulting total/age normalization, dated supply anchor and links to the 2023 person/head bridge. Existing source hashes and measured/external/derived classifications must be carried forward or explicitly replaced. No particular replacement closure is recommended here.

The historical evaluator independently hard-codes `birth_to_entry_conversion=1./2.1` at [baseline line 279](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/run_e5f_matched_pf_baseline.py:279); changing the initializer alone would leave an inconsistent historical law. After 2023 the person/head operator instead advances top-code-adjusted births through annual sex shares, survival, migration and headship ([lines 792–831](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/run_e5f_perfect_foresight_person_demography.py:792)). Do not conflate those closures or automatically replace the later law with the old queue rule. These obligations apply to both two-stage and joint calibration. Once a two-stage initial economy is fixed, scalar shock trials can reuse its contracted old accounting; structural changes in the joint design require recomputing its derived objects.

### Third design: impose an empirical nonstationary initial distribution

The present initial condition is
\(g_{2007}(b,t,i,j,z,n,c)=w_jg^{SS}(b,t,i,j,z,n,c)\): [age reweighting, lines 514–529](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/run_e5f_open_population_transition.py:514) preserves **every within-age joint state** and the original total mass. It is not an empirical joint distribution. The [PF initial-state interface](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/run_e5f_perfect_foresight_transition.py:73) can hold a nonstationary distribution, but the current selected initializer does not construct one.

An empirical replacement needs the joint distribution of liquid financial wealth/debt \(b\), inherited renter/owner housing product \(t\), market \(i\) (one active market), household age \(j\), persistent income state \(z\), lifetime parity \(n\), and current dependent-child state \(c\). The exact axes are visible in the [entrant constructor](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/tools/run_dynamic_population_transition.py:371) and [solver geometry](/Users/tommasodesanto/Desktop/Projects/Fertility/Fertility_Spring26/tmp/e5f_matched_pf/code/model/intergen_eqscale_seq_optimized/solver.py:2358). Additional requirements are consistent mapping into the 120 wealth nodes and owner products, a treatment of latent income and any enabled readiness state, 3+ parity versus resident dependents, initial people/head sex-age composition and maternal exposure, and pre-2007 birth vintages/entry flows. Current separate CPS parity, PSID wealth and ACS housing marginals do not supply those joint correlations. A statistical matching or structural imputation model would need its own sources, uncertainty and validation. The existing feasibility/projection gate still applies to the supplied distribution.

Imposed initial stocks cease to be behavioral fits: initial wealth cannot identify patience/bequests through stationary accumulation, initial parity/childlessness cannot identify fertility costs/scales through past fertility choices, and imposed tenure/product shares cannot identify past ownership choices. Current flows, choices conditional on inherited states and later transitions can still identify parameters, but sufficient informative restrictions must be demonstrated anew. Initial prices/supply must also be contracted; an empirical distribution does not itself identify housing supply or clear the market. Computing initial moments directly from imposed data and counting them as fitted restrictions would create spurious identification.

Finally, this design is only **forward propagation conditional on already-solved policies**. Perfect foresight still requires backward solution from the future value boundary and a joint price-path root; using fixed current policies or one-date expectations instead would change the user's expectation specification. It saves the old stationary initialization (about five minutes at the observed sequential point), not the roughly 42–56-minute full-path evaluations. It is an economically possible third architecture, but no complete empirical state builder or identifying target contract has been verified today.
