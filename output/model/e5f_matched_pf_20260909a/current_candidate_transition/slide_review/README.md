Fertility data: existing published annual national female TFR and four-year arithmetic means. A model decision in2007 is compared with births2008–2011; the2023 decision is not relabeled as fertility observed in2023. The initial2.1 segment is the completed-fertility normalization, not a separately reconstructed period-fertility observation. The model fertility index sums birth flows divided by model household age masses, retains the top-parity adjustment, and covers ages18–45; the existing female/household interpretation and age-support approximation remain unchanged. No measurement or target changes were applied. The preference path is announced in2007 and declines linearly through2023, then is constant; fertility can respond in2007 through anticipation even though the contemporaneous preference parameter has not yet fallen.

Housing comparator: `code/data/Spatial_aggregate_withmicrodata/output/national_householder_housing_path/national_householder_housing_path.csv`. Metadata verifies national ACS annual samples, HHWT weighting, household heads18–85, valid tenure/room observations. Saved ownership and room ratios are independently recomputed from numerators and denominators. National household comparisons are supplemental validation, distinct from the metro initial calibration targets. Literal recorded rooms have different historical top-coding and are not a harmonized growth series. Model room units are uncapped physical housing demand divided by household mass; ownership is owner mass divided by household mass. Household totals and age shares are imposed during the historical bridge, so their reproduction is not an endogenous model fit. No resident-person historical series is inferred from household counts.
