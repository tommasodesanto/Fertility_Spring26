## Recommended empirical design

Use an approximate pre-decline stationary economy, announce the entire preference path in 2007, and observe 2023 on that transition. Anchor initial fertility in 2003–2006 and initial wealth in the 2005/2007 PSID waves. Retain the reviewed pooled housing-response restrictions and measure the existing 2012–2023 metro housing observations over the corresponding transition window. Estimate the common parameters jointly with the shock amplitude. This is an initial economy disciplined by early observations and pooled structural restrictions; it is not a complete fit of every variable to a 2007 cross-section.

The preference path remains

\[
\psi_t=\psi_0+\Delta\psi\min\{1,\max\{0,(t-2007)/16\}\}.
\]

Households know the path from its announcement, including the maintained flat continuation after 2023. The fitted preference change is a conditional driver for scenarios, not a finding about the cause of the fertility decline. Keep it fixed across policy counterfactuals. Its utility-unit amplitude is estimated through the model; it is not numerically equal to a decline in births per woman.

The table covers all eleven currently searched coordinates, plus the initial preference level, which is currently recovered from a normalization. “Main discipline” identifies the most relevant variation; it does not assert exclusive or proven identification.

## Other restrictions remain visible

The **recent-parent ownership gap, 16.7662 percentage points**, remains a hard joint restriction on the housing, tenure and fertility blocks in the proposed system. There is no extra parameter uniquely associated with this row. Its empirical groups are oldest resident own child under four versus no resident own child, whereas the current model compares any dependents with never-parent history. This mapping must be resolved explicitly. Calendar averaging does not fix it.

Retain the existing later fertility observations in the joint fit: CPS June 2024 completed fertility **1.918** and childlessness **0.188**, plus NCHS cohorts 1979–1984 mean first-birth age **26.044627** and age-30+ share **0.2603274**. They constrain how the initial population and common parameters evolve under the same shock. They are not annual 2023 fertility rates. The proposed early timing, early childlessness and early parity-shape rows add evidence; they do not silently demote the established later rows. Model cohort measurement still requires verification against their exact birth-year/age windows.

Thus the proposal has **18 scalar observations**: the existing twelve, with the wealth ratio's proposed initial-era replacement, plus the initial and late period-rate anchors, two early timing observations, early childlessness and early one-child share. The initial preference level is recovered from its anchor, leaving the existing eleven searched coordinates. Merely counting observations does not prove identification. Check the weighted moment Jacobian at the matched PF solution, especially the fertility-scale/cost block and the saving/bequest block. An older calibration's full-rank Jacobian cannot certify this new system.

Do not add conditional mean fertility as another independent restriction alongside mean completed fertility and childlessness: \(E[N\mid N>0]=E[N]/(1-P(N=0))\). Exactly-one conditional on motherhood contains different parity-shape information. Nor should zero-parity share be counted separately from childlessness. A covariance-based weight matrix must exclude exact redundancies; new moments must not inherit unrelated old SEs or synthetic scales.

## What the newly recovered old data tell us

The early CPS pool gives mean completed fertility **1.85661** when capped at five (**1.87838** uncapped), while the proposed early period-rate anchor is **2.0605**. The former describes the past births of women already aged 40–44; the latter summarizes current age-specific birth rates. They need not agree in the data. Our stationary approximation cannot simply claim to fit both as the same quantity.

My proposal gives the initial level to the **period-rate anchor**, because the requested exercise conditions on a decline in current birth rates. Keep early completed fertility as a disclosed check on the stationary approximation. This is a modeling choice, not a data correction. The 1.85661 extraction is new evidence, never an existing hard target being removed. Initial childlessness and parity shape still add useful discipline, but their cohort histories can also expose the limits of the stationary approximation. If these initial restrictions prove incompatible, diagnose that conflict rather than drop them automatically.

We have more local data than earlier source discovery suggested: CPS June 2004/2006/2008 and older natality raw files are present. What is missing is a certified initial-era builder for some measures, not necessarily the underlying data. In contrast, the active matched-metro ACS geography genuinely does not provide a ready 2007 target. New nationwide ACS extraction would change the target universe and requires an explicit decision.

## What must be finished today before a launch is defensible

1. **Approve the economic measurement choice:** initial period fertility anchor, early fertility shape, pooled structural restrictions, and housing observed on the transition. The exact proposal is above; the current production contract remains unchanged.
2. **Finish the small empirical receipts:** formal CPS old/new comparability and uncertainty; PSID initial ratio uncertainty; declared weights/covariances for new timing/rate observations. All raw sources needed for the CPS/PSID work are local. Do not reuse a full-pool SE for a two-wave estimate.
3. **Verify the model observations:** female exposure and annualization for period fertility; first-birth flows for period timing; dated cohort stocks; ACS date pooling and both family-group definitions. These are code/measurement tasks, not cluster-capacity problems. National fertility versus metro housing remains an explicit maintained geographic approximation.
4. **Freeze the new target/weight contract only after those checks.** No joint calibration should launch with an incomplete observer or an unannounced target change. The PF equilibrium/horizon certification is a separate numerical prerequisite, unchanged by this empirical inventory.

Today the concrete target map, published period rates, preliminary early CPS/timing points and exact early wealth ratio are available. The proposed objective is **not yet launch-ready**. No numerical solve was needed or run for this audit.
