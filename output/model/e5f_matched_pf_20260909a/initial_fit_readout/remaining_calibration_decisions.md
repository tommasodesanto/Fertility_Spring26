# Remaining decisions for an honest initial calibration

Prepared September 11, 2026. Bounded, read-only economic and measurement assessment for the lead. This note changes no source, empirical target, observer, weight, job, or calibration status. It incorporates the completed recent-parent checkpoint probe 17362130; the older statements that no full-checkpoint observation exists are superseded by that receipt, not silently edited here.

**An initial minimum-distance calibration does not require exact agreement between a coarse model and every empirical population or a design-exact covariance matrix.** It does require a fixed, defensible observation rule, all retained scored rows, an explicit finite weighting rule, and the existing numerical and identification checks. The recent-parent flow observer can supply a defensible model analogue of the retained ACS target under its stated approximations. Its newly measured wrong sign is a fit problem, not evidence by itself of a defective observer. Existing search results still have null actual weights and no complete activated objective; this note does not promote them.

**What the author has already approved.** The [approved plan, lines 3–12][plan] authorizes implementation, gated launches and autonomous useful follow-up rounds. It expressly approves the new early target design conditional on certification of its builders, observers, weights and population identities. [Lines 68–94][plan68] retain twelve scored moments for nine jointly searched structural coordinates, plus the separate 2.1 normalization of the initial preference level. They retain both family-size rooms and recent-parent ownership, keep the national-fertility/PSID versus metro-housing approximation visible, and preserve the old late-target contract. They do not impose the old CPS completed-fertility mean 1.856608 as another equality to 2.1. The author also maintains the approximate child-at-home interpretation; exact adult-child co-residence is not a newly required model state. Historical preference estimation comes after initial calibration, with its own exposure, calendar and closure requirements.

Conditional approval leaves methodological work for the lead; it does not reserve every age projection or covariance convention for another author decision. But line 92 is binding: an invented empirical scale chosen merely to start a search is prohibited, and unresolved provisional weights cannot become the calibrated benchmark or support production policies by changing their label. A scientifically justified working minimum-distance specification can complete the approved certification task. If the lead cannot justify the remaining scales, the existing authorization supports continued provisional diagnostics only.

**Classification of the outstanding issues.** Here “repair” means correcting a provable mismatch with the declared estimator or a failed accounting identity. “Approximation” means a transparent representation of an empirical object within the maintained model. “Author choice” means changing the approved economic or target design.

**Recent-parent observation: a defensible maintained approximation, including with a wrong sign.** The retained empirical object is

\[
P(\mathrm{owner}\mid NCHILD>0,\ ELDCH<4)
-P(\mathrm{owner}\mid NCHILD=0),
\]

for ACS 2005/06 household heads aged 30–55 in the prescribed geography and structures. `ELDCH` is the oldest resident own child's age: the treatment group requires all resident own children to be under four. Its control includes former parents whose children no longer live at home. The verified target is 0.16289550916123285, with reported SE 0.006079524679464666. See the [group derivation and original source contract][flowdesign].

The implemented analogue selects actual current births from households with no pre-birth dependents, including eligible former parents and the maintained readiness states. It transports that selected household mass through the same actual current location and tenure choices, then compares it with current households without dependents. Under the explicit synchronized post-fertility observation, surviving children already present before the current birth date are at least four, so this selection implements the model-time oldest-child criterion. The uniform four-year **head-age overlap** is separate from an annual birth/interview reconstruction. It must not be described as a unique implication of the aggregate calendar bridge.

The necessary requirements are actual conception probabilities and original risk sets; correct first/continuation handling; no multiplication of selected household mass by a representative top-parity birth count; ownership after current transport, including destination tenure for movers; both never-parent and former-parent controls; valid mass and group denominators; and replay/partition identities. These are testable. Literal annual interviews, adult children returning home and multiple births within four years are unmodeled features. Declaring their approximation is appropriate; requiring exact reconstruction of all of them would replace the approved approximate-model exercise with a richer project.

The [completed full-checkpoint probe][probesummary] reports thirteen compiled startup tests, 641 verified source files, zero household/equilibrium solves and exact equality across two observations. I independently checked equality of the two saved pass JSONs. The replay and group-accounting errors are at numerical precision. Selected-birth ownership is 0.4989822429 and current-empty ownership is 0.5522258288, yielding **−0.0532435859** against **+0.1628955092**, a model-minus-target gap of **−0.2161390951**. Former-parent controls have ownership 0.6379636518 versus 0.4477396657 for never-parent controls. These components show that correctly including former parents matters economically; they do not establish the cause of the entire miss. The [complete baseline target and parameter tables][baseline] supply the surrounding fit; the [joint-smoke tables][smoke] retain every restriction and all parameter bounds, but have not yet incorporated this separate new observer.

This supports certification of **implementation under the named approximation**. It does not certify an exact annual ACS mapper, strong identification, or good fit. There is no evidence yet that timing explains the wrong sign, so it must not be dismissed as a definition problem. The lead can accept the named analogue within the existing conditional approval and retain the negative residual in the complete objective. Actual checkpoint measurement of the joint candidate, and ultimately every candidate being scored, remains necessary; a baseline observation cannot be copied to changed parameters.

**A defensible weighting rule need not be statistically optimal.** Let \(\theta\) contain the nine structural coordinates, and let \(\psi_0(\theta)\) satisfy the separately enforced 2.1 normalization. A possible mathematical form is

\[
Q(\theta)=\sum_{k=1}^{12}
\left[\frac{m_k(\theta,\psi_0(\theta);\mathcal O)-d_k}{s_k}\right]^2,
\qquad 0<s_k<\infty,
\]

where \(\mathcal O\) is the frozen set of observation rules and every \(d_k\) is a retained approved empirical or external restriction. This expression describes the task; **it supplies no selected scales or weights**. A positive definite working weight matrix defines a minimum-distance compromise even without a full empirical covariance matrix. Under model misspecification, the weights affect the selected parameter compromise; they therefore need substantive justification and sensitivity. Efficient-GMM, standard-error or overidentification-test claims require additional assumptions and uncertainty analysis. Twelve rows and a locally rank-nine Jacobian are not those claims.

CPS annual approximate indicator SEs follow the official generalized-variance formula \(s_y=\sqrt{b_y p_y(1-p_y)/X_y}\), with fertility coefficient 2016 and the appropriate all-women or mothers denominator. For fixed observed annual pooling shares \(\alpha\), a working pooled variance is

\[
\alpha^2s_{04}^2+(1-\alpha)^2s_{06}^2
+2\alpha(1-\alpha)\rho s_{04}s_{06}.
\]

The recovered \(\rho=0\) and \(\rho=1\) candidates are respectively .003751255/.005305036 for childlessness and .004307419/.006091130 for exactly-one among mothers. Correlation one is an upper candidate **within this fixed-base formula**, not a proven bound on the complete pooled ratio design variance. Cross-year area sampling and random mother-denominator weights remain qualifications. The lead may document an approximate working convention and sensitivity; a person bootstrap cannot manufacture missing PSU/stratum information. The official approximations are documented in [Census June 2004][cps04] and [June 2006][cps06], and the original-sample computations are in the fertility assessment.

For NCHS, registration counts have no probability-sampling error conditional on the recorded population. NCHS nevertheless discusses event-process random variation and nonsampling error; see [Births: Final Data for 2006, printed pages 96–99][nchs]. The recovered conditional process SEs (.002249892 years and .000168244 for the share) measure one statistical concept. The annual 2003–06 SDs (.084567369 years and .008492262) measure temporal dispersion; leave-one-year-out shifts measure window sensitivity. None is automatically the right error scale for a stationary coarse-age model. A temporal-discrepancy rule is a defensible candidate if its purpose, assumptions and sensitivity are declared, but it cannot be presented as a sampling SE. The old .15/.01 pair remains explicitly synthetic old-cohort borrowing. Complete counts neither force zero residuals nor justify infinite inverse weights.

State the CPS pooling convention, NCHS process/discrepancy interpretation and inherited external/synthetic restriction treatment. Pin a complete fingerprint before the scored search. Selecting a justified working rule lies within the plan's conditional authorization; selecting convenient numbers solely to launch, or promoting still-unresolved provisional profiles, does not. If this scientific step remains incomplete, call subsequent work a provisional diagnostic search.
4. **Evaluate the complete criterion and inspect identification.** Recompute fit, contributions and local sensitivities with all twelve rows and the fixed rule; examine weak directions and numerical accuracy before extending the bounded joint search. The existing nineteen-case panel has rank nine but scaled condition numbers about 1242–1894 and asymmetric responses. This warrants care and sensitivity, not either a claim of proven identification or an automatic finding of underidentification. Report full target and parameter tables, near-bound status and all numerical gates.

These steps can support the honest name **“initial minimum-distance calibration under the stated observation and weighting approximations”** once completed and reviewed by the lead. That name does not waive line 92 or certify a production policy baseline. Historical horizon, fiscal, geographic and entry closure certification likewise remain separate policy gates. A new author decision is needed if the lead proposes changing the already approved scientific design or bypassing those conditions, rather than completing them.
