## Implication

There are two concrete alignment problems to bring to the author: recent parenthood versus any current dependent child in the ownership gap, and any-age co-resident children versus dependent-count states in the room gap. The existing target scalars and provenance are solidly traceable; that does not make the model measurement operators identical to their empirical counterparts. The model's aggregate household-with-dependents calculation is internally well defined, but neither these targets nor CPS fertility stocks directly validate its national level.
