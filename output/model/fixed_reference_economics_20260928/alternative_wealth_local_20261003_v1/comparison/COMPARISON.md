## What the alternative wealth numerator measures

The [PSID builder and receipt](../../../wealth_numerator_match_20261002/README.md) reproduce the old pooled ratio 6.926584 and calculate `ratio_c_noveh = 4.45838713455674`. Both use the same 2005/2007 reference-person sample, ages 18–85 for the net-wealth numerator and ages at most 65 for gross labor earnings, and positive PSID individual weights. The new numerator removes business/farm equity, other real estate and vehicles; it retains home equity, cash/bonds, stocks/retirement assets, catch-all other assets and other debts. The component identity was checked against PSID total net worth. The excluded categories account for about 35.6% of the original weighted net-wealth aggregate (17.3%, 14.3%, and 4.1%, respectively). No new standard error was estimated; keeping the old numerical weight is a controlled sensitivity, not a claim of unchanged sampling precision.

The [model accounting routine](../../../../../code/model/intergen_housing_fertility_optimized/solver.py) forms wealth from signed net financial balances plus owner house value and divides it by annual gross labor earnings. Excluding business, other real estate and vehicles moves the empirical numerator toward those model assets. Exact coverage is **not** established: the PSID catch-all other-assets category remains in the new empirical target without a separately identified model counterpart, and the model's financial balance is not decomposed into the survey's asset and debt components. Model housing value is a room-product value rather than observed PSID home equity item by item. The survey ratio and model observer share broad age and annual gross-earnings concepts, but the inherited PSID income filter, stock/flow dating and model pension denominator retain the documented approximations. Therefore a lower empirical ratio alone is not evidence that population sampling or model wealth accounting is fully representative.

The **bequest** issue becomes more visible with the numerator change. The unchanged 2007 SCF target divides a child-directed estate-flow proxy by broad signed net worth. The model divides its all-child-state estate flow by the same beginning wealth stock used for its wealth/earnings moment; the saved chain-02 observer reports annual estate flow 0.033396 and wealth stock 4.860941. The experimental PSID wealth numerator excludes asset categories that may enter the SCF estate denominator, while the model estate observer does not impose child-recipient eligibility. The two empirical ratios therefore do not yet have a demonstrated common balance-sheet definition. No second target was changed here.

## Parameters, identification and search limits

There are ten positively weighted targets and ten free parameters in each arm, with the same nominal bounds. Count alone does not establish informative rank or separate identification. In particular, annual discounting \(\beta\) and the bequest taste scale \(\theta_0\) can both affect aggregate wealth; the wealth level and annual bequest-flow ratio jointly discipline that block, while older wealth dispersion is currently validation only and has a measurement mismatch. The free \(\beta\) estimates are 0.967034, 0.966319 and 0.964495, and \(\theta_0\) estimates are 0.158316, 0.100974 and 0.106175. These movements do not identify either mechanism on their own. The original-timing \(h_P=2.6\) contacts its upper bound; post-interest old-target \(h_P=2.59376\) lies near it; new-target \(h_P=2.50802\) is interior. The two fertility dispersion parameters are flagged near the low end of their very wide [0.02, 50] search intervals in all arms. [The complete parameter table](complete_parameter_comparison.csv) preserves all fixed, derived and free records.

The timing search had 24 matched starts in each arm, six hours and at most 250 evaluations per chain; 46/48 chains passed selected native and exact-repeat verification, while chain 23 in each arm had no admissible point. The wealth-target search had ten starts, at most two hours 45 minutes and 250 calls per chain; all ten passed native verification, and each stopped at its evaluation budget. These unequal search designs and missing convergence certificates preclude a claim that the new wealth target is unreachable. Before adopting or discarding it, the unresolved work is to reconcile the empirical/model asset mapping and bequest denominator, estimate uncertainty for the new PSID statistic, and test the wealth–bequest parameter block's local rank or profile with a deliberate common-budget design. This readout makes no target, weight, entry-distribution, or model change.
