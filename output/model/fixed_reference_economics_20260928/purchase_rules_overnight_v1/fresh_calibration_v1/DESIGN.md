# Fresh hard and quarter 80% calibration campaign

This is a new 24-chain multistart search, not a continuation of an old chain's clock or objective budget. It holds fixed the isolated hard/quarter purchase-rule engines, 80% financed share, the 14-moment target contract (10 scored moments), target weights, 10 free coordinates and their approved bounds, renewal-price root, $N_0=1$ normalization and derived $H_0$. The fits are experimental; this campaign does not adopt either rule as the paper baseline.

The six centers are fresh selected-point postchecks: hard Torch continuation 11 (loss 97.011220), local 51 (98.290682), original Torch 14 (100.326885); quarter local 54 (51.556036), original Torch 35 (51.970163), continuation 32 (52.852165). Each center supplies four deterministic starts: its exact coordinates, $h_P-0.025$, $h_P+0.015$ clipped to 2.595, and a joint small perturbation of $\psi_{child}$ (0.003), $\chi$ (0.02) and $\theta_0$ (0.005) with alternating signs. `design.json` pins all 24 distinct vectors, center receipt references, initial prices, bounds, target/weight fingerprints, and original source hashes. This neighborhood design revisits promising but nonconverged areas without repeating the failed broad Latin-hypercube spread.

Each independent chain has one CPU, 24 GiB, at most four hours from actual start, at most 250 objective calls, and a separate 900-second selected-point postcheck reserve. The campaign also stops by 18:00 New York, October 2, 2026. Maximum exploratory size is $24\times250=6{,}000$ objective calls plus at most 24 final postchecks, or 96 core-hours at the wall cap. At the recent median of about 149 seconds per objective and 225 minutes of search time per chain, a rough realized count is 2,160 objective calls across 24 chains; throughput is not guaranteed. The reviewed 32-native-call/300-second candidate limit, native gates, latest/best checkpoints, invalid-candidate censor, full 14-target/31-parameter/17-plot final postchecks, and fail-closed source/contract checks remain in force. A search point without a fresh final postcheck is not a calibrated result.

`test_design.py` checks all 24 arm/center routes and vectors, target/bound/source pins, the exact candidate sequence passed→candidate cap→passed, and the deadline stop with zero native solves. Mounted `preflight_torch.sh` authenticates the frozen source and executes initializer-only slots 0 and 12, requiring zero lifecycle solves. Once approved, `stage_torch.sh` stages source files and pins; `launch_torch.sh` runs array tasks in separate result roots. Progress, `search/latest.json`, `search/best_so_far.json`, `search/cases.json`, and a `launcher_terminal.json` remain per slot. The Sol monitor reads these without mutating jobs.

Submission is recorded in `submission_receipt.json`: Slurm array **19040483**, tasks 0–23 at up to 24 concurrent jobs. Both mounted initializer preflights passed before submission. Immediately after launch, Slurm reported the array pending. The source under the staged root is immutable for this run; any revision requires a new named campaign and new receipt.
