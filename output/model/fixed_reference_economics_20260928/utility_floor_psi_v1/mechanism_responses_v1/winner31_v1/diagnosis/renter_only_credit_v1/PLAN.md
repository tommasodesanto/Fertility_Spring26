Status: **implemented and completed experimentally**. The three-cell local packet and results are in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). Lead review preceded numerical execution. The point is the selected-repeat-verified winner chain 7/case 0173, with original-weight loss 31.28400725566496 and fixed price \(q_0=0.719168368828958\). Keep its 31 reported parameter fields, 14 target rows, 120×9 grid, entrant distribution, preferences, fiscal and estate rules, and housing closure fixed. This is an experimental credit comparison, not a calibration or production adoption.

## Economic contract

In the control, `credit.bind_engine_credit(P, "corrected", 0.0)` imposes renter saving \(b'\ge0\), including death-risk ages. The treatment removes only that fixed renter unsecured limit. At each age, income state, and family state, the renter's lower saving bound becomes the first wealth-grid node from which every positive-probability next-income/child-state continuation is feasible. If death has positive probability, net renter estate wealth must be nonnegative, so \(b'\ge0\) at that age. Reject non-upper-interval support and all-dead continuation columns; the existing `V > -1e9` classifier is approximate and cannot certify support beyond the finite grid. No debt interest premium or new lender/estate counterparty is introduced.

Buyer origination remains \(\phi=0.8\): the current-income-adjusted transaction threshold is \(x+y/R\ge-0.8pH\), with \(x=b+(1-\psi)pH_{\mathrm{old}}-pH_{\mathrm{new}}\), and the buyer's final mortgage floor remains \(b'\ge-0.8pH_{\mathrm{new}}\). Existing owners retain native DUE: \(b'\ge\max\{\min(b,-0.8pH),-(1-\psi)pH\}\) when death is possible, omitting the estate term otherwise. Buyer and stayer kernels, purchase-income timing, sale proceeds, inherited entry, transfers, prices, and grid are unchanged. Renter relief can affect future owner choices through ordinary continuation values; it does not change the owner borrowing rule itself.

## Why the existing natural-credit switch cannot be used

`code/model/refactor_lab/engine/household.py` rejects `native_solvency_credit=True` together with native DUE. That global switch also replaces the owner saving floor, removes the buyer down-payment/mortgage gate, and applies death/continuation infeasibility to all tenure cells. The earlier `lifetime_repayment_only` experiment explicitly disabled DUE and cleared buyer gates. It is not a renter-only comparison.

## Stop conditions and limitations

Stop before implementation if the isolated copy cannot preserve the owner/buyer branches byte-for-byte, or if `child_maturation_mode`/solver structure differs from the authenticated winner. Stop numerics on any exact-control mismatch, all-dead or non-upper renter support, support trace gap, occupied lower-grid boundary, negative renter death estate, transaction/budget/fiscal/KFE failure, or deadline/call cap. The continuation cutoff and finite \([-12,3000]\) grid prevent a natural-borrowing-limit certificate even after a passing bounded diagnostic.
