its weight in the loss \eqref{eq:q-loss} and its contribution to the loss at
the estimates. Table~\ref{tab:app-jacobian} reports the elasticity of each
targeted moment to each estimated parameter, from a 1\% change in the
parameter, solving the equilibrium each time.
