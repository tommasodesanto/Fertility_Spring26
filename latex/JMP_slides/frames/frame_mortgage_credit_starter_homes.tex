\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{3pt}
\item No quantum effect. The LTV constraint does not enter the user cost of space; the marginal household rents at $b=0$. Branch values rise by $+0.008$ (child) and $+0.007$ (no child).
\item Tempo effect only. Liquidity brings forward births of new buyers and leveraged owners in the first period; along the GE path births are $-0.3$ to $-0.8\%$ thereafter.
\item Composition. One fifth of childless renters become owners of two-room units below the parent floor of 2.6; childlessness at 46--49 rises from 19.0\% to 20.2\%, progression to a second child is unchanged.
\item Without the two- and four-room rungs the same LTV change leaves completed fertility unchanged. Credit changes tenure, not the price of a child.
\end{itemize}
