\begin{frame}
\frametitle{The fix: delete the entry logit, fix the immigration quota}
\textbf{Problem.} The previous entry-adjusted population responses ($+9$--$10\%$)
were $\sim 90\%$ driven by an unidentified entry taste scale ($\lambda=2$) on a
nearly saturated margin ($q^*=0.97$): a free parameter, in utility units, on a
knife edge.

\medskip
\textbf{Fix.} Retire the outside value and logit. Hold fixed the native
retention rate $\bar R=(1-s^{out})E_0/B_0=0.969$ and the absolute outside
inflow $\bar M=s^{out}E_0$ (quota rationing, $s^{out}=0.169$). Solve
$S E_0=\bar R\,S\,B_0(\text{policy})+\bar M$ jointly with the house price and
the balanced budget. Population then responds only through births, with
renewal multiplier $(1-s^{out})/s^{out}=4.92$, damped by housing scarcity.
Baseline is bit-identical; zero recalibration.

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Labels and open decisions}
\textbf{How to read the population numbers.} Long-run stationary comparisons,
not forecasts: renewal arithmetic closes half the gap in roughly a century
(generational decay $\rho=0.831$, $g=26$--$30$y $\Rightarrow$ $97$--$112$y;
an age-structured variant with uniform birth timing gives $\sim 230$y).
Welfare statements stay per-household; never total-utilitarian across
different populations.

\medskip
\textbf{Anchor decision (new, from last night's ACS measurement).}
Among age-18--22 household entrants, recent immigrants are only $3.7\%$
(all variants $2.9$--$9.9\%$) --- below the $14.3\%$ feasibility floor.
Real immigration sustains the population at \emph{older} arrival ages, so:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Age-aggregated anchor (recommended):} $\bar M$ = total net
  international migration in entrant-household equivalents
  ($\sim 0.17$--$0.22$, brackets the provisional $0.169$); age-18 entry is an
  explicit compression of arrival ages. Results stand as computed.
  \item \textbf{Literal entry-age anchor:} $3.7\%$ --- then the closure is
  infeasible at every certified arm and requires multi-age immigrant entry
  or a stronger fertility block.
\end{enumerate}

\medskip
\textbf{Fertility-fit stress test.} Feasibility requires
$B_0/E_0\ge 0.831$. The repaired-E5 chain-6 winner delivers $0.788$
(completed fertility $1.78$ vs.\ $1.918$) and is screened out; the floor/tilt
arms pass with $3\%$ slack. A 16-chain multi-start on the unchanged contract
is running overnight to test whether a better basin exists.
\end{frame}
