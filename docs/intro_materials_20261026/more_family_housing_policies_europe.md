# Additional child/family-linked housing policies (verified 2026-10-26)
Raw downloads: /private/tmp/claude-501/intro_dl_G/ . All quotes below were seen in raw text of the cited page. Translations are mine.

## 1. Greece, "Spiti mou II" (My Home II) -- VERIFIED
- What: Enacted RRF-funded programme of low-interest first-home loans via banks for ages 25-50 (Hellenic Development Bank, Min. Social Cohesion and Family, Min. Finance). Application window closed (final enrolment date 31.05.2026; loan contract by 31.08.2026). Succeeded by a reported "Spiti mou 3" (press only, UNVERIFIED).
- Key numbers: max loan EUR 190,000 (max 90% of value, value <= EUR 250,000); 50% of loan interest-free (RRF part), rest bank rate. Couples' max income EUR 35,000 + EUR 5,000 per child (was 28,000 + 4,000 after Nov 2025 amendment); single-parent EUR 39,000 + 5,000 per child beyond the first. Child terms: interest on bank part subsidised 50% for third-child (tritekno) or large (polyteknos) families.
- Quote (portal, Greek; my translation in brackets): "Ζευγάρια ... €35.000 (+ €5.000 * αριθμός τέκνων)" [couples ... EUR 35,000 (+ EUR 5,000 x number of children)]; "Αν ο δικαιούχος είναι i) τρίτεκνος ή πολύτεκνος ... το ποσό των τόκων για το μέρος που παρέχεται από την Τράπεζα, θα επιδοτείται κατά 50%" [if the beneficiary has three or more children ... interest on the bank-provided part is subsidised 50%].
- Also (Ministry/Greece 2.0 notice on KYA amendment): "35.000 ευρώ (από 28.000 ευρώ) ... προσαυξάνεται κατά 5.000 ευρώ ανά παιδί (έναντι 4.000 ευρώ ανά παιδί)".
- URLs: https://stegasi.gov.gr/programs/spiti-mou-ii/ (Ministry portal; accessed 2026-10-26) ; https://greece20.gov.gr/spiti-mou-ii-nees-rythmiseis/ (Nov 2025 amendment notice; page date not in raw text, Nov 2025 per press). Base KYA 189973/16.12.2024 (FEK B 6894) per secondary source, UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Italy, Fondo di garanzia mutui prima casa (CONSAP / MEF) -- VERIFIED
- What: Enacted public guarantee (since Law 147/2013) on first-home mortgages up to EUR 250,000, restricted to priority groups, including young couples, under-36s, single-parent households with minor children, and large families by number of children. Priority regime extended to 31 Dec 2027.
- Key numbers: guarantee 50% of principal by default; higher guarantee possible for priority categories (loan > 80% of price). Large families: three children under 21 and ISEE <= EUR 40,000; four children, ISEE <= 45,000; five or more, ISEE <= 50,000. CONSAP's CEO (press piece, 20 Jun 2025) says the Budget Law raised the guarantee to up to 90% for families with at least 5 children and ISEE up to EUR 50,000 and extended the priority regime to 2027 (statement of an official, not statute text).
- Quote (Italian; my translation): "nuclei familiari con tre figli di età inferiore a ventuno anni e con ISEE non superiore a 40.000 euro annui" [households with three children under 21 and ISEE not above EUR 40,000 a year]; "nuclei familiari monogenitoriali con figli minori" [single-parent households with minor children].
- URLs: https://www.consap.it/fondo-prima-casa/ (page last updated 23 Oct 2026); https://www.consap.it/sanasi-darpe-fondo-casa-sostiene-natalita-e-famiglie-numerose/ (20 Jun 2025).
- Note: page also reports Law 2 July 2026 n. 116 (converting DL 66/2026, "Piano Casa") added disability categories from 3 Aug 2026 (not child-related).

## 3. Spain, ICO state-guarantee line "jovenes y familias con menores a cargo" -- VERIFIED
- What: Enacted (Council of Ministers agreement 9 May 2023; RDL 5/2023 art. 191; amended 13 Feb 2024; ICO convenio published in BOE 7 May 2024). State guarantees part of mortgage for first home; separate programme for families with dependent minors.
- Key numbers: guarantee covers up to 20% of principal (25% if energy rating D or better); net worth cap EUR 100,000; family programme income cap 4.5 x IPREM plus 0.3 x IPREM per dependent minor (single-parent +70%); youth programme age <= 35.
- Quote (Spanish; my translation): "El aval del MIVAU cubrirá hasta el 20 % del principal de cada préstamo" [the MIVAU guarantee will cover up to 20% of each loan's principal]; "Dicho límite o suma se incrementará en 0,3 veces el IPREM por cada menor a cargo" [the limit is raised by 0.3 x IPREM per dependent minor].
- URL: https://boe.es/boe/dias/2024/05/07/pdfs/BOE-A-2024-9128.pdf (BOE no. 111, 7 May 2024, resolution dated 5 May 2024).

## 4. Germany, KfW 300 "Wohneigentum fuer Familien - Neubau" -- VERIFIED (terms as of Stand 12/2025)
- What: Enacted federal programme (Min. for Housing, Urban Development and Building as principal): subsidised KfW loan for families with minor children building or buying new, climate-friendly owner-occupied homes. Start date June 2023 and the Oct 2023 income-limit increase appear only in secondary sources (UNVERIFIED here).
- Key numbers: loan EUR 170,000-270,000 depending on standard and number of children: 1-2 children 170,000 (220,000 with QNG), 3-4 children 200,000 (250,000), 5+ children 220,000 (270,000). Taxable household income <= EUR 90,000 with one child plus EUR 10,000 per additional child; at least one child under 18; no existing owned home.
- Quote (German): "90.000 Euro bei einem Kind, zuzüglich 10.000 Euro je weiterem Kind"; "ab 5 Kinder: maximaler Kreditbetrag 220.000 Euro".
- URL: https://www.kfw.de/PDF/Download-Center/F%C3%B6rderprogramme-(Inlandsf%C3%B6rderung)/PDF-Doku-mente/6000005060_M_300_WEF.pdf (Merkblatt, Stand 12/2025). Local: /private/tmp/claude-501/intro_dl_G/kfw300.pdf

## 5. France, Pret a taux zero (PTZ) -- VERIFIED
- What: Enacted, state-backed interest-free loan complementing a mortgage for first-time buyers; income ceiling and (per ministry) loan amount depend on household size and zone. Since April 2025 extended to new homes nationwide until 31 Dec 2027 (per Economie.gouv, secondary to this check; not in my raw pull -- UNVERIFIED).
- Key numbers: ceilings for a 1-person household EUR 49,000 (zone A/Abis) vs EUR 102,900 for 4 persons and 161,700 for 8+ (zone A); zone C 28,500 vs 59,850 (4 persons). Household-size scale is roughly 1, 1.5, 1.8, 2.1 times the single ceiling, i.e. child-adjusted.
- Quote (service-public.fr, French; my translation): "Ce montant dépend du nombre de personnes logées et de la commune de votre futur logement" [this amount depends on the number of persons housed and on the municipality of the future home]; "Les ressources doivent être inférieures à un plafond, qui dépend de la zone ... et du nombre de personnes destinées à l'occuper" (ecologie.gouv.fr).
- URLs: https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F10871 (checked 15 Sep 2026); https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/pret-taux-zero-ptz

## 6. Czech Republic, SFPI loan programme "Vlastni bydleni" (formerly "Pujcky pro mlade") -- VERIFIED for 2021 terms; current terms UNVERIFIED
- What: Enacted state loan (Statni fond podpory investic) for people under 40 who are married/registered partners or permanently care for a child under 15; interest rate falls with each child.
- Key numbers (MMR release 5 Oct 2021, effective 1 Jan 2022): loan up to CZK 3.5m (house) / CZK 3m (flat), 30 years, 90% of eligible costs; rate = EU base rate minus 0.2 pp per child under 15, floor 1% p.a., fixed 5 years. A press search says SFPI later raised the age cap and limits; I could not verify any later terms (sfpi.cz returns a firewall block 466 to curl).
- Quote (Czech; my translation): "snizena o 0,2 procentniho bodu za kazde dite do 15 let veku" [reduced by 0.2 percentage points for each child under 15] (accents dropped; original has diacritics: "snížené o 0,2 procentního bodu za každé dítě do 15 let věku").
- URL: https://mmr.gov.cz/cs/ostatni/web/novinky/klara-dostalova-program-vlastni-bydleni-nabidne-vy (5 Oct 2021, Ministry for Regional Development).
