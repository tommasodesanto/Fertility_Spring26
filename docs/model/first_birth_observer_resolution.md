# First-birth housing observer: minimum resolution protocol

**Purpose.** Specify the smallest defensible bridge between the author-selected PSID first-birth event study and the model's housing response, without changing the target, model, or any saved run. This is an implementation design only; no estimate is produced here.

## Decision and status

The September 24 author-labeled **final empirical version** is the household-panel estimator: one woman who is the current reference person or spouse per single-family-unit household-year; PSID longitudinal weights; first biological birth from the full relationship history; confirmed childless women as controls; entry at the first adult PSID observation; corrected official room counts with non-room codes missing; person and survey-year fixed effects; age and education controls; and person-clustered uncertainty. Its headline is +3/+4 versus −3/−2, **1.025 rooms (SE 0.073)**. The receipt labels it `final_empirical_estimate_horizon_pending_author`; this establishes the chosen empirical version, not an adopted model horizon or target contract.

The **1.465-room** figure is a different object: the all-adult estimator restricted on the treated side to women already reference persons or spouses in the −3/−2 baseline window (A2h). It is not the household-design estimate. The chronology matters: on September 25 at 00:47Z the author asked whether the answer was “like 1”; at 00:48Z they said 1.47 felt enormous and they needed to think more. Later the September 25 paired-run instruction explicitly set 1.465 for both experimental arms and said it superseded a brief 1.400 instruction. That instruction authorized the experimental target for that run; this memo does not infer a revocation. The choice of a future empirical/model target contract is separate from authorization to use 1.465 in that experiment. Keep that run and its target fingerprint frozen. The earlier 0.720246 row is also frozen and is not silently replaced by this memo.

The native observer is a behavioral branch contrast, not an estimator: it takes childless settled risk-set states, assigns a first birth to one copy, holds the other childless, advances both one four-year period, permits a treated continuation birth, and compares realized rooms at the destination. It uses stationary first-birth-flow weights, rather than PSID cohort weights. Its saved output is aggregate branch mass/rooms and continuation-birth mass, not respondent-level panel histories. Thus no existing scalar (including 0.987 or 1.044 from saved floor/share diagnostics) is a matched estimate of the PSID regression.

|---|---|---|---|
| Unit | Woman; one current reference person or spouse per single-family-unit household-year | One model household | Model households correspond to H only after acknowledging that every model unit is already an independent household; it cannot reproduce leaving a parental dwelling or the row-by-row head/spouse selection. |
| First birth | First biological child in full RELCHI1–20 history; no earlier than first adult observation | First-birth flow from childless states with settled readiness, weighted by the model's attempt/fecundity process | Preserve each definition. Do not use first child record as a substitute or condition on post-birth household status. |
| Controls | Women confirmed to have zero children in full relationship history | Identical origin states cloned and mechanically held childless | These are different comparison groups. A simulated-panel estimator must select never-birth histories as controls; retain same-state branches only as a separate mechanism diagnostic. |
| Clock | PSID event time in annual waves through 1997 and biennial waves thereafter; two-year event windows | Choice and housing updates at four-year model dates; no within-period birth date | No exact window match follows from calling both horizons “four years.” A within-period event-date convention must be declared and checked. |
| Outcome | Official annual PSID room variables aligned to interview year; codes 9/99/98–99 cleaned by year, zero retained | Realized model rooms at discrete dates, after endogenous location/tenure/housing choice | Convert neither to a common unit by scaling; run the same estimator on simulated observations. |
| Window / controls | +3/+4 against −3/−2; person and year FE, `i.AGEREP i.EDUYEAR`, PSID IW weights; cluster by person | Destination at t+1 against a same-state childless copy at t+1 | Horizon, counterfactual and weights differ. ## Minimum matched-observation protocol

1. **Freeze the empirical reference.** Use `sa_rooms_first_birth_v2.do`, its `target_receipt.csv`, and full `window_path.csv` as the empirical reference, not the experimental 1.465 target. Preserve H's cohort support, sample rules, event windows, PSID IW weights, official rooms, and full birth histories. Do not edit the existing result package. The original survey panel can be reconstructed by `prepare_first_birth_rooms_v2.do` from the local PSID-SHELF panel and official year-specific PSID room variables; the prepared `analysis_sample.dta` itself was written to a private `/tmp` path and is not the durable source.

2. **Make one education/age bridge rather than dropping controls on one side.** The H regression controls for exact reported age and education; the active optimized model has no education state, and its age is on four-year cells. Therefore preserve H unchanged as the accepted empirical estimate, and create a separately labeled *bridge estimator* on the PSID panel that uses four-year age cells and omits education. Run the same event-study equation on simulated model observations with those same age cells and no education. Report the bridge's difference from H's 1.025 as an empirical specification sensitivity; never call the bridge estimate the accepted target without author approval. This makes the unavailable schooling dimension visible instead of silently treating model and data equations as identical.

3. **Generate model histories, not another branch mean.** From the latest lead-verified selected checkpoint's frozen policies, transitions, stationary entry distribution and fertility shocks, simulate a seeded panel of household histories from adult entry through the end of the model fertility horizon. Retain household ID, model age cell, calendar period, fertility/parity/dependent state, tenure, rooms, income, survival, and first-birth date. Keep all subsequent births. Define treated histories by realized first biological birth and controls by zero births through the end of fecundity, paralleling the data's full-history definition. Give each history its model probability weight; do not substitute PSID weights for model probabilities. The latest completed comparison point identified by the lead is nightpair `worker05point04` (source commit prefix `d5ef71`); pin its full checkpoint path and hash from that control receipt. If the September 26 four-arm utility run completes and its selected point passes lead review, use that current verified checkpoint instead. Do **not** use the older September 23 B-floor checkpoint as the default measurement source; it is historical evidence only. Verify the current source manifest and policy objects before read-only measurement; this is not a household/equilibrium solve or recalibration.

4. **Replay the observed interview pattern jointly.** Attach actual H-sample interview-year patterns to simulated histories by sampling complete person-level patterns within first-birth cohort and baseline eligibility, using the original PSID panel's joint support. Do not reconstruct a regression from marginal cohort-by-event support: it does not encode which event times, missing rooms, births, and survey weights belong to the same woman. Keep the actual annual/biennial calendar and the same event windows and event-study estimator. Generate enough independent panels to report simulation uncertainty separately from the empirical clustered SE.

5. **Pin the within-period birth-date convention before calculating fit.** The model realizes fertility at a four-year decision date and has no identified within-period birth date. Main bridge: place birth at the model decision date, which preserves current timing. Sensitivities: midpoint and a predeclared uniform offset within the four-year cell. Hold housing at the model's chosen level until its next decision date. Report the complete pre- and post-birth event path under each convention. These are measurement conventions, not estimated facts; if the bridge statistic or pre-period path changes materially across them, the four-year model does not identify the annual PSID event path and the observer remains unresolved.

6. **Estimate and report the identical reduced equation.** On both PSID bridge data and simulated histories, estimate the H cohort-interacted event study with person and calendar-year FE, four-year age-cell controls, the same baseline and post-event windows, and weights appropriate to each population (PSID IW versus simulated model mass). For the model report both the estimator contrast and the existing same-origin branch (D_1) as distinct objects, plus the event-time pre-path, first-birth cohort weights, and second-birth share by the +3/+4 window. Never rescale (D_1) into the regression coefficient using a timing identity; that identity depends on the within-period date convention and treatment-effect heterogeneity.

This is a descriptive auxiliary comparison in the model economy. The PSID Sun–Abraham coefficient has a causal interpretation only under its parallel-trends and no-anticipation assumptions; endogenous never-birth controls in simulated data do not make those assumptions true by construction.

## Definite gaps and scope

- **No person-level model paths are saved.** The lunch observer receipts contain aggregate branch means/mass and second-birth mass only. A history simulator or equivalent event-tagged micro-panel measurement pass is required.
- **No selected checkpoint is local.** The local normalized seed is not the selected calibration. The latest completed selected point is nightpair `worker05point04`; use it unless the current four-arm utility comparison completes and its selected point passes lead review. Pin the full path and hash from the responsible control receipt before any measurement.
- **No education state exists in the active model.** A full H-equation match is impossible without adding education to the model or changing the empirical conditioning equation. The proposed bridge addresses this symmetrically and remains a distinct estimand.
- **No within-period birth date is modeled.** The chosen date rule is an observation convention only. Sensitivity that affects the headline materially is a stop signal, not license to choose the convention that fits.
- **No common household-formation process exists.** Model households are independent households; the H design's relationship-to-reference-person screen varies by interview. The H-to-model interpretation is therefore conditional and descriptive. Do not claim it reproduces household formation or transitions out of parental homes.
- **No author-approved future model-observer horizon/target contract is recorded.** The September 24 H estimate is the documented empirical reference, but the mapping from model transition to event-time contrast remains open. The authorized experimental 1.465 calibration target and frozen 0.720246 contract remain untouched unless changed by an explicit decision.

No search, objective, solve, target, or source edits.
3. **Empirical gate (existing data/code):** reproduce v2 H from its existing builder/estimator without modifying outputs; compute the bridge estimator from the same prepared observations; assert identical cohort/sample/interview support apart from the declared age/education adjustment. Save both under a new observer-resolution output path.
4. **Model gate (measurement only):** simulate one panel per timing convention from the frozen policies; estimate the same bridge equation. Validate that first-birth cohort weights sum to one, simulated mass is conserved, interview-pattern assignment is exact, the declared never-birth controls remain childless, and the branch (D_1) reproduces the saved observer value on the checkpoint.
5. **Decision table:** show empirical H=1.025 (SE .073), empirical bridge estimate and sample/support change, model bridge contrast with Monte Carlo SE, (D_1), pre-period coefficients, and timing-convention sensitivity. Keep 1.465 labeled as the authorized experimental run target; do not claim it is adopted as the future empirical/model contract absent a separate decision.

**Completion criterion:** by Sunday, the package contains a signed measurement contract, source/checkpoint hashes, reproducible scripts and receipts, empirical H and bridge estimates, model bridge estimates with Monte Carlo uncertainty, pre-path and timing sensitivities, and a clear pass/fail statement on whether the model can speak to the chosen event-study estimand. A pass requires exact sample/interview-pattern support checks and timing-robust results; otherwise report the observer as unresolved and preserve existing targets/runs. This criterion does not authorize target adoption or production recalibration.
